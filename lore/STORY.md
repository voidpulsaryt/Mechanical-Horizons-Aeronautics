# Mechanical Horizons: Aeronautics — story bible

Reference for anyone adding quests or lore. Everything in the quest book should agree with this page.

## The premise

Three hundred years ago the **Horizon Guild** raised **the Aerie**, a city of brass and canvas that flew above the clouds. It was held up by the **Horizon Engine**, which had a captured singularity at its core. The singularity was held in balance by every discipline the Guild knew: gears to spin it, steel to cage it, crystal to tune it, industry to feed it, the Deep Works to contain it and the Vault to remember its shape.

One night the Guild pushed the Engine past every limit. That night is called **the Overclock**. The fall that followed is called **the Grounding**. The Aerie came down in pieces, the disciplines scattered, and the meteorite craters across the world are really fragments of the Vault.

The player is an apprentice who finds the logbook of **Master Wright Ada Corrin**, the Aerie's chief navigator.

## The mystery (what the player slowly learns)

1. **Official story:** greed. The Consortium wanted more output, and the Guild gave it to them.
2. **Truth, part one:** the Engine had been failing for **eleven years**. The Choir heard it singing flat. The Deep Works' containment readings drifted. The Consortium's ledger showed falling output despite rising fuel. Every report reached Guildmaster Rook, and every report vanished.
3. **Truth, part two:** Rook was not greedy, he was desperate. The Deep Works had a theory that the singularity would steady itself above the **horizon line**, a theoretical altitude no ship had reached. The Overclock was a climb, not a profit grab.
4. **Truth, part three:** Ada was the navigator who plotted the climb. She knew the numbers did not work, and she signed the course anyway. The logbook is her confession.
5. **Truth, part four:** in the last of the eleven seconds, Ada cut the Engine's heart loose from the city, so the singularity would not take the whole Aerie with it. The city fell instead of vanishing. That is why there were survivors.
6. **The twist:** "I did not make it down" is literal. The heart, caught in levitite, drifted up to the old **pouring yards**, the floating foundry islands from Log IV, and Ada went with it. She is still up there, keeping it stable, waiting for someone to build a ship that can reach her.

The final quest ends with an invitation: *come find me. I left the lamp on.*

## The cast

| Name | School (mod) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Ada Corrin** | Windwright, chief navigator | Narrator. Plotted the Overclock's course. Still alive above the pouring yards. |
| **Guildmaster Halvard Rook** | Once the best Windwright alive | Ordered the Overclock to save the city. Hid the failing Engine for eleven years. Lost in the fall. |
| **Master Tobin Fell** | Cogwrights (Create) | Ada's teacher. Left the Aerie a year before the Overclock because the Engine "sounded wrong". Retired to the ground rail towns. |
| **Foreman Bram Holt** | The Foundry (Immersive Engineering) | Never trusted the Aerie and refused to sign the refit order. Cut the mooring-tower power lines that night, so the surge never reached the ground towns. |
| **Cantor Liesl Marr** | Conduit Choir (Powah) | Heard the Engine singing flat for years and filed reports that disappeared. Begged on the bridge that night. |
| **Director Imra Vess** | The Consortium (Modern Industrialization) | Paid for the refit in full and in advance. Installed the Quantum Upgrade. Walked out of the wreckage in quantum armor. |
| **Ori** | Founder of the Deep Works (Oritech) | Long dead. Wired an off switch into every Deep Works machine. |
| **Warden Kael Brask** | The Deep Works (Oritech) | Removed Ori's off switches on Rook's order. |
| **Archivist Senna Quell** | Vaultkeepers (AE2) | Sealed the Vault's records in the Mysterious Cubes before the fall. The truth is buried in the craters. |

The smaller guilds: the **Windwrights** (Create Aeronautics), the **Helmwrights** who built every ship's bridge (Aeroworks), the **Thrust Guild** who chose raw thrust over lift (Create Propulsion) and the **Horizon Exchange**, the Aerie's merchants (Lightman's Currency).

## Where the story is told

- **Horizon Logs chapter:** the spine. Prologue, thirteen logs and an epilogue, each unlocked by a milestone in another chapter. Acts: I–IV learning, V–VIII the schools and the warning signs, IX–XIII the reveals.
- **Mod chapters:** a few story beats each (opener, key milestones, the capstone), in the voice of Ada's margin notes. Each school's named character appears in its own chapter.
- **Lore files:** `branding/lore/*.txt` (one `QUEST_ID|line` per row); the Horizon Logs text and chapter subtitles live in `branding/lore/base/en_us.snbt`. Run `branding\lore\apply.ps1` after editing.

Recurring details to keep consistent: **eleven** (seconds of climb, years of failure, quantum hulls built), the **clock tower stopped at eleven seconds past the hour**, Ada's **cracked left goggle lens**, the **lamp**.
